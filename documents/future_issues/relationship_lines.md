# Relationship Lines on Map

## Objective

Visualize certain relationship types as dotted lines between pins on the map. Lines are color-coded by relationship type: red for `at_war_with`, green for `allied_with`, blue for `trades_with`. Lines are only drawn when both the source and target pins are visible on the current map with their layers active. This gives the user an immediate spatial understanding of how entities relate without leaving the map view.
